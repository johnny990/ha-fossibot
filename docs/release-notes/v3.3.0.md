# v3.3.0 — Battery power and energy counters for protocol V1 stations (F7200)

## ✨ Added

**Battery Power** (V1 input register 90). The net power of the pack, signed:
positive while charging, negative while discharging. The device reports the
opposite sign; the integration flips it so that a positive number means the
battery is filling up, which is also how the two counters below are defined.
**Battery Charge Power** and **Battery Discharge Power** (disabled by default)
are the two non-negative halves.

**Battery Charge Energy** and **Battery Discharge Energy** (kWh,
`total_increasing`). The device keeps no such counters, so the integration
integrates the split battery power on every refresh (left Riemann sum over the
poll interval) and restores the running total across restarts. Add them in
the Energy dashboard under *Battery systems* as "energy going in to" and
"energy coming out of" the battery; no template or Riemann-sum helpers are
needed. An interval longer than 10 minutes between two samples (restart,
reconnect) is dropped rather than guessed.

**AC Input Voltage / Frequency** on V1 stations (input registers 8 / 9, grid
side; 0 with no grid).

**Diagnostic entities for still-undocumented V1 fields**: Charge Priority
(holding 88, raw, also a number for experiments), UPS Mode (holding 85, raw),
Grid Charge Auto / Immediate / Custom (state-word bits 8-10) and *Unmapped
Inputs (raw)* (disabled by default), which carries the input words with no
known meaning as attributes so live comparisons can name them.

## ⚠️ Notes

- The energy counters start at 0 when the entities are created and only count
  from then on; they are not a lifetime figure from the device.
- While the station is unreachable the coordinator keeps the last readings;
  the counters treat a reading older than 10 minutes as unknown and skip it.

## 🧪 Tests

`tests/test_registers_v1.py` checks the battery power sign and split against
the captured frames (814 W and 173 W out of the pack) and a charging word
(64932 = 604 W in); `tests/test_energy.py` covers the integration step and its
skip rules; the entity-definition tests bind the new counter table to its
entity class and keep its keys apart from the decoded ones.
